# What's New in 1.0.75

**Spotter Tools Pro  |  iOS, Android and Windows**

![](/images/whats-new-1.0.75.jpg)

**See more of every storm.** The new **Prism lens** shows velocity, correlation coefficient and more right inside the reflectivity you are already looking at. 3D radar draws height lines right on the storms, plus sunlight and hail cores. Warning polygons now play back with the radar loop, a new command bar puts your most used products one tap away, and Surface Obs adds about 25,000 weather stations.

> **On Android?** 1.0.75 follows right after Google finishes reviewing 1.0.74, which is still with them.

> **New to these features?** There is a step by step guide for each one at [spottertools.pro/1.0.75/howto](/1.0.75/howto)

## Prism lens

A circle you drag over a storm that shows a different radar product inside it, so you never have to flip back and forth.

- **Look through the reflectivity.** Inside the lens the map shows velocity, storm-relative velocity, correlation coefficient, spectrum width or reflectivity, while everything outside stays as it was.
- **Up to four at once.** Split the lens into 2, 3 or 4 slices, each with its own product, for a velocity, SRV and CC look at the same spot side by side.
- **Move it and size it.** Drag the lens anywhere, resize it with the knob (or pinch on a phone, Ctrl + scroll wheel on Windows), and tap a slice's label to change its product.
- **Always current.** Each label shows the product and how old its scan is, and turns amber if it falls more than 2 minutes behind the map.
- **Loops and archive too.** The lens keeps working while a loop plays and in archive mode, matching each frame to the nearest scan of each slice.

> **The lens shows the lowest tilt** and works over any single site radar product. It steps aside in 3D, dual view and with the map tilted.

## 3D radar: height lines on every storm

- **Heights on the storm itself.** Contour lines every 10,000 ft wrap the storms with numbers on the strongest cells, and echo-top tags mark up to 24 cells, so you can read how tall a storm is at a glance. The old height ruler pole is gone.
- **Sunlight and shadows.** Storms are lit from the southwest and cast a shadow on the ground, so towers, anvils and overshooting tops stand out. Colours stay true to your palette.
- **Hail cores.** The 60+ dBZ cores show through the storm's shell in your palette. Turn them on with the new **Cores** toggle.
- **Cleaner highlights.** Debris and rotation get colours no reflectivity palette uses, and rotation only shows as a real column (rotating in two or more tilts, at least 1.5 km tall), so weak-cell noise drops out.
- **Longer 3D loops,** up to 6 hours, and loop exports now include the 3D volume frame by frame.
- **A 3D time line** under the scan time shows the volume's own time, in amber when it is behind.
- **Smarter starts.** 3D shows the newest finished scan right away instead of waiting for the one in progress, stays on when you switch radars, and tells you when a clear-air scan is too shallow for 3D.

## Warnings play back with the loop

- **The warnings in effect at each frame.** Single site and national loops now draw the warning polygons as they were at that moment: a warning appears on the frame it was issued, changes shape with each update, and disappears when it expired or was cancelled.
- **Tornado, severe thunderstorm, flash flood, marine, extreme wind, snow squall and dust storm warnings, plus special weather statements.** Watches and advisories stay as they are now.
- **The newest frame is always live,** and archive mode and loop exports use the same timeline.

## A new command bar

- **A one tap shortcut to the most used products.** One floating bar at the top with the logo, search, **Single Site**, **National**, **Models**, **Satellite** and **Outlooks** tabs, and your beacon status. It replaces the layer button in the bottom right corner.
- **Everything else is where it was.** All the other layers and overlays are still in **Map layers**.
- **The product's controls hang right under it.**
- **Phones get a compact one row version,** and tablets and PCs fit the full bar by measuring the screen instead of guessing.
- **A bright red close button** on every product panel, so turning a product off is easy to find.

## Clearer product panels

- **Inspector everywhere.** The crosshair value readout now has its own **Inspector** chip on national radar, models, mesoanalysis and observations.
- **Know how old it is.** National radar shows the MRMS source and "N min ago"; mesoanalysis and observations show the local valid time and age. Each turns amber when the data is overdue.
- **Models follow new runs.** The run menu now starts with **Latest**, so picking a run no longer pins you to it by accident. A pinned run shows a pin, and a **New run** badge appears when a newer run is out. The settings gear is now a **Display** chip.
- **Outlooks, organised.** The product list is grouped into Storms, Rain & flooding, Fire, Winter and Long range, every product has one time chip ("Day 1 · Today", "2-6 PM"), and the info icon now says when it was issued.

## Surface Obs

- **About 25,000 more stations.** The METARs layer is now **Surface Obs**: airport reports plus fire weather (RAWS), government and state mesonet stations, and optional citizen (CWOP) stations, from NOAA MADIS.
- **Pick your stations** with the new **Stations** chip. Each type has its own small coloured dot on the map.
- **New readings:** wind gust and relative humidity. Tap a station for its network, elevation, humidity and precipitation.
- **Zoomed out, it stays tidy** with the same density as before; more stations fill in as you zoom.

## Tropical

- **Google DeepMind's AI hurricane model** now draws in the model spaghetti as its own yellow track.
- **Every model track is labelled** with its name in its own colour (NHC, GFS, ECMWF, HAFS-A, Google AI and more), above the NHC track.
- **Fixed:** a broken outlook area near the date line that painted a yellow band across the Pacific.

## Soundings: a smarter READ

- **What happens next.** The READ now looks at the hours after the one you picked and tells you when the threat peaks, whether the cap erodes or builds, and whether instability is growing or fading.
- **Convective temperature,** and whether daytime heating alone can get storms going.
- **Moisture check.** It warns when the instability depends on a couple of degrees of dewpoint ("Fragile instability ... watch surface dewpoints").
- **More honest wording** about caps, hail and wind, matching the hazard chips.

## Map labels

- **State names always show,** spelled out in full, instead of disappearing behind radar sites and chaser icons at national zoom.
- **Major cities always show** too.
- **New City density setting** (Sparse, Normal, Dense, Maximum) in **Settings**, under **City labels**.

## Radar

- **Scan time in the legend.** The legend now shows the scan's time, age, VCP and a small sweep that fills in as the next tilt arrives. The age turns amber at 10 minutes and red at 20.
- **Tornado ID at KFSX, KMAX and KMSX.** These radars scan below the horizon, which kept Tornado ID from ever reading them. Fixed.

## Loop export

- **Faster.** On Windows, loop exports are 2 to 3 times quicker.
- **Windows saves to your Videos or Pictures folder** (in a Spotter Tools Pro folder) with a **Show in folder** button, and a new **Full** size exports at your screen's full resolution.
- **Models, MRMS and air quality loops** no longer repeat a frame.

## Windows

- **A smooth radar beam.** The sweep line could stall at 2 frames a second; it now runs at your screen's refresh rate.
- **Much lighter loops with warnings on.** Loops that played with nationwide advisories showing used a lot of CPU and memory; now they use about a third of the CPU and a fraction of the memory.
- **Switching radars stops the loop** instead of playing the old site's tracks over a blank map.

## Fixes and polish

- **iPhone and iPad:** fixed a crash in background refresh, the most common crash in 1.0.74.
- **CarPlay:** fixed a crash when starting a trip on iOS 26.
- Fewer rare crashes when the map reloads its style or asks for location twice.
