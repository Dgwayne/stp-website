# What's New in 1.0.72

**Spotter Tools Pro  |  iOS, Android and Windows**

![](/images/whats-new-1.0.72.jpg)

**Your settings now follow you.** Sign in once and your map, layers, radar setup, alert zones and more are the same on your phone, your tablet and your PC. The national radar also gets cleaner by default, gains its own rain, snow, sleet and ice view, and tropical model tracks are fixed and fuller. Everything below ships on all three platforms unless a section says otherwise.

> **New to these features?** There is a step by step guide for each one, for phones, tablets and Windows, at [spottertools.pro/1.0.72/howto](/1.0.72/howto)

## Settings Sync

Set the app up once. If you use Spotter Tools Pro on more than one device, every one of them now gets the same setup.

- **Signed in, it just works.** Sign in to your free Spotter Tools Pro account in Settings and sync turns on by itself. Change your map style on your phone and it is sent within seconds; your PC picks it up the next time you open it or come back to its window, or straight away with **Sync now**.
- **What comes along:** your map style and theme, which layers are on and their options, radar settings such as gate filters, colour palettes (including your own imported .pal files), favorite radars, alert zones and notification choices, your map marker and your home location.
- **Choose what syncs.** Six groups you can untick on any device: Map style and theme, Layers and layer options, Radar, Alerts and zones, Marker and icon sizes, and Home location and other. Keep different layers on your PC and your phone if you like.
- **Icon sizes stay sensible.** Marker and icon sizes sync between devices of the same kind, phones and tablets together and computers together, because the same slider looks different on a 6 inch screen and a 27 inch one.
- **Your phone stays your phone.** Some things never leave the device: passwords and Spotter Network sign-in, your beacon, location sharing, the alerts on and off switch, Chaser Mode and screen settings.
- **Never mid-use.** Changes from another device land when you open the app or come back to it, or when you tap **Sync now**, never while you are in the middle of something.
- **Already set up on two devices?** The first time you sign in on a device that has its own settings, the app asks which set to keep.
- **Your data, your call.** Turn sync off on any device, or delete the saved copy from your account entirely.

> **Settings Sync needs a Spotter Tools Pro account.** It is free and optional: sign in with Google, Apple or an email address under Settings, Spotter Tools Pro Account. The app works exactly as before without one.

## A cleaner national radar

- **Quality controlled by default.** The national radar now opens on **Base Reflectivity (QC)**, built from NOAA's MRMS data with birds, insects and ground clutter removed. Those returns sit at the same strength as light rain, so no filter slider could ever clear them, and on many nights they painted a false wash of light green across the East.
- **Your choice sticks.** Every device switches to the QC version once. If you prefer the original, pick **Base Reflectivity** again and the app remembers it.

> **Longer loops need the original.** The QC product and Precip Type loop up to 2 hours. For 3, 6 or 12 hour national loops, switch to **Base Reflectivity**.

## Precip Type for the national radar

1.0.71 brought rain, snow, sleet and freezing rain colouring to single site radar. Now the whole country gets it.

- **The national mosaic, coloured by type.** Pick **Precip Type** in the national radar's product list and every echo is drawn on a rain, snow, sleet or freezing rain colour scale.
- **Built on the clean mosaic.** It uses the quality controlled radar, so a bird migration is never painted as light snow.
- **Updated every 15 minutes** from the HRRR model, and each loop frame is coloured from its own time.
- **The inspector reads the type** at any spot, along with the reflectivity.

> **Precip Type covers the lower 48 states,** where the HRRR model runs. The radar cannot see the temperature between the cloud and the ground, so the type comes from the model and the strength comes from the radar.

## Tropical

- **No more lines around the world.** Storms crossing the 180 degree line in the Pacific drew their model tracks, cones and paths the long way round the globe. They now stay where they belong.
- **Every model's latest run.** Spaghetti plots now show each model's newest track from the last 12 hours, instead of a single cycle that left out the ensembles and the slower models.
- **Tracks stay current.** They are checked for updates far more often, and if an update fails the last good tracks stay on the map.

## Fixes and polish

- **Single site Precip Type is for live radar only.** In archive mode it now steps aside for Reflectivity, because the model data it needs is only kept for the last few hours, and older scans were painting grey.
- **Smoother panning on phones** with an MRMS product on the national radar. Every pan used to unpack the whole national image again just to find it was already drawn.
- **Searching Settings for "sync"** now takes you to the sync controls.
