# What's New in 1.0.74

**Spotter Tools Pro  |  iOS, Android and Windows**

![](/images/whats-new-1.0.74.jpg)

**Radar in your car.** Spotter Tools Pro now runs on **CarPlay** and **Android Auto**, with live radar and warnings on the car's own screen. Phones get spoken turn-by-turn directions over live radar, the map shows which storm chasers are streaming right now, and a new Share button turns any screen into a branded picture.

> **On Android or Windows?** Your last update was 1.0.71, so this one also brings everything from 1.0.72 that was waiting: Settings Sync, a cleaner national radar, national Precip Type and the tropical fixes. iPhone users got those, and turn-by-turn directions, in 1.0.73.

> **New to these features?** There is a step by step guide for each one at [spottertools.pro/1.0.74/howto](/1.0.74/howto)

## CarPlay (iPhone)

Spotter Tools Pro is now a CarPlay navigation app, so it can put a live map on your car's screen.

- **Live radar on the car screen,** under the roads, with warning polygons, storm cells, storm reports and radar site markers.
- **Pick what you see.** **Layers** switches between Reflectivity, Velocity, Storm-relative velocity, Correlation coefficient and the National composite, turns warnings on or off, picks a dark or light map, and mutes spoken directions. **Sites** chooses a radar, or Automatic to follow the nearest one as you drive.
- **Turn-by-turn on the car.** Tap search to find a town, ZIP code or address, or tap **Go** for recent places and storms nearby, then **Go** again for spoken directions on CarPlay's own turn cards. A trip started on your phone shows up in the car, and **End** in the car ends it on both.
- **Warnings come to you.** Car alerts pop up on the CarPlay screen.
- **Dashboard and instrument cluster** views show a simpler map with your route and warnings.

> **CarPlay needs an iPhone and a CarPlay car or head unit.** Searches find towns, ZIP codes and street addresses, not business names.

## Android Auto

Spotter Tools Pro now opens in the Weather section of Android Auto.

- **Live radar on the car screen,** from the nearest radar as you drive, with warning polygons from coast to coast.
- **A status card** shows the radar, product and scan age, and the nearest warning in its own colour, or how soon an approaching storm reaches you.
- **Step back through scans.** The history button shows up to five earlier scans, one per tap, then returns to live by itself.
- **Storm cells and reports.** Cells show their forecast track and an arrival estimate to your position; storm reports appear as lettered markers. Tap any cell, report or warning for details, then **Navigate** to hand the spot to Google Maps, Waze or your car's navigation.
- **Layers** offers Reflectivity, Velocity, Storm-relative velocity, Correlation coefficient and the National composite, plus a map style that follows the car's day and night.
- **Heads-up alerts.** Warnings from the phone app's alerts appear on the car screen while connected.

> **Android Auto shows the latest scan, not a loop,** because Google does not allow animation in Weather apps while driving. Turn-by-turn directions stay on the phone; tap Navigate to send a spot to your navigation app.

## Turn-by-turn directions (phones)

New on Android. iPhone users have had directions since 1.0.73, and this update keeps the spoken turns going with the iPhone locked.

- **Three ways to start.** Long-press the map and tap **Directions here**, tap the car button on any search result, or pick one of your **Recent destinations**.
- **A clear preview** with the time, distance and main roads before you tap **Start**.
- **A turn banner** across the top of the map with the next turn, its distance, the road and your arrival time, over live radar.
- **Spoken prompts** that lower your music instead of stopping it, with a **Voice** button to mute them.
- **Automatic rerouting** after a wrong turn, and the map follows you at street level while the screen stays awake.

> **Directions are for phones and tablets,** not Windows. Watch the road, not the radar.

## Live Chaser Positions

- **See who is live.** A new map layer marks featured storm chasers who are streaming on YouTube right now and sharing their position on Spotter Network.
- **Tap to watch.** Tapping a marker opens their live stream in the app.
- **Partners stand out.** Spotter Tools Pro partner chasers get a gold-ringed marker with their photo and are pinned to the top of the Live Chasers list, starting with Jim McGonigle.
- **Turn it on** in **Map layers**, **Live Chaser Positions**, or with **Show on map** in the Live Chasers panel.

## Share button

- **One tap to share what you see.** A new **Share** button in the bottom bar captures the screen you are on, map included, and adds a footer with the Spotter Tools Pro logo, spottertools.pro and the date and time.
- **The footer goes below the picture,** never over your legends, and opens your phone's or PC's share menu.

## Settings Sync (new on Android and Windows)

Set the app up once. If you use Spotter Tools Pro on more than one device, every one of them now gets the same setup.

- **Signed in, it just works.** Sign in to your free Spotter Tools Pro account in Settings and sync turns on by itself. Change your map style on your phone and it is sent within seconds; your PC picks it up the next time you open it or come back to its window, or straight away with **Sync now**.
- **What comes along:** your map style and theme, which layers are on and their options, radar settings such as gate filters, colour palettes (including your own imported .pal files), favorite radars, alert zones and notification choices, your map marker and your home location.
- **Choose what syncs.** Six groups you can untick on any device: Map style and theme, Layers and layer options, Radar, Alerts and zones, Marker and icon sizes, and Home location and other. Keep different layers on your PC and your phone if you like.
- **Icon sizes stay sensible.** Marker and icon sizes sync between devices of the same kind, phones and tablets together and computers together, because the same slider looks different on a 6 inch screen and a 27 inch one.
- **Your phone stays your phone.** Some things never leave the device: passwords and Spotter Network sign-in, your beacon, location sharing, the alerts on and off switch, Chaser Mode and screen settings.
- **Never mid-use.** Changes from another device land when you open the app or come back to it, or when you tap **Sync now**, never while you are in the middle of something.
- **Already set up on two devices?** The first time you sign in on a device that has its own settings, the app asks which set to keep.
- **Your data, your call.** Turn sync off on any device, or delete the saved copy from your account entirely.

> **Settings Sync needs a Spotter Tools Pro account.** It is free and optional: sign in with Google, Apple or an email address under Settings, Spotter Tools Pro Account. The app works exactly as before without one.

## National radar

- **Quality controlled by default.** The national radar now opens on **Base Reflectivity (QC)**, built from NOAA's MRMS data with birds, insects and ground clutter removed. Those returns sit at the same strength as light rain, so no filter slider could ever clear them, and on many nights they painted a false wash of light green across the East.
- **Your choice sticks.** Every device switches to the QC version once. If you prefer the original, pick **Base Reflectivity** again and the app remembers it.

> **Longer loops need the original.** The QC product and Precip Type loop up to 2 hours. For 3, 6 or 12 hour national loops, switch to **Base Reflectivity**.

### Precip Type for the whole country

- **The national mosaic, coloured by type.** Pick **Precip Type** in the national radar's product list and every echo is drawn on a rain, snow, sleet or freezing rain colour scale.
- **Built on the clean mosaic.** It uses the quality controlled radar, so a bird migration is never painted as light snow.
- **Updated every 15 minutes** from the HRRR model, and each loop frame is coloured from its own time.
- **The inspector reads the type** at any spot, along with the reflectivity.

> **Precip Type covers the lower 48 states,** where the HRRR model runs. The radar cannot see the temperature between the cloud and the ground, so the type comes from the model and the strength comes from the radar.

### Low and mid level rotation together

- **Both rotation levels on one map.** Pick **Low + Mid** under Rotation in the national radar's product list. Each level keeps its own colours, and where both are spinning the map draws diagonal stripes of the two, so stacked rotation jumps out.
- **Smooth rotation colours,** shown in the units forecasters use, with low level rotation in warm colours and mid level rotation in violet so the two never look alike.

### Smoother colour scales

- **Hail size, echo tops, VIL, rain totals and precip rate** now blend smoothly instead of stepping between bands, with weak values drawn as a quiet grey wash so ordinary rain stays calm.
- **Your palette everywhere.** The reflectivity layers in the national product list, such as the low, high and isotherm composites and the hourly maximums, now use your own reflectivity colour table and gate filter instead of an old default.

## Radar fixes

- **Archived velocity loops keep every scan.** Level 2 velocity loops in archive mode could skip 30 to 60 minutes on nights when the radar kept changing its settings. They now keep every scan.
- **One bad scan no longer cuts a loop short.** A single scan that failed to download used to end a Level 2 loop at that point and drop every newer frame.
- **Single site Precip Type is for live radar only.** In archive mode it now steps aside for Reflectivity, because the model data it needs is only kept for a few hours.

## Tropical

- **No more lines around the world.** Storms crossing the 180 degree line in the Pacific drew their model tracks, cones and paths the long way round the globe. They now stay where they belong.
- **Every model's latest run.** Spaghetti plots now show each model's newest track from the last 12 hours, instead of a single cycle that left out the ensembles and the slower models.
- **Tracks stay current.** They are checked for updates far more often, and if an update fails the last good tracks stay on the map.

## Fixes and polish

- **My location finds you fresh.** Tapping the location button now asks for a new GPS fix instead of reusing a cached one, which on iPhone could land on yesterday's spot.
- **Spoken turns with the iPhone locked.** Directions keep talking when the phone is locked or another app is in front.
- **Windows: icons and layers on the first launch after an update.** On a busy first launch, markers could be missing and switched-off layers could show until the next start.
- **Searching Settings for "sync"** now takes you to the sync controls.
