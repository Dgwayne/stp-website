# What's New in 1.0.76

**Spotter Tools Pro  |  iOS, Android and Windows**

![](/images/whats-new-1.0.76.jpg)

**Go live, and know which storm to watch.** Windows gets **Broadcast Mode**, a full-screen stream view with an alert crawl, breaking-alert takeovers, scenes that rotate your saved views and a warning jump that flies to new warnings on its own. Everyone gets NOAA's **ProbSevere** storm probabilities with ProbTor tags, **pop-out cameras** that float over the map, **lightning coloured by strength** with a tap card, and a much louder **Tornado Emergency** alarm.

> **New to these features?** There is a step by step guide for each one at [spottertools.pro/1.0.76/howto](/1.0.76/howto)

## Broadcast Mode (Windows)

A stream-ready view for anyone who goes live during severe weather. Open it from the map menu or press **F11**, and press **Esc** or **F11** to leave.

- **Full screen with an alert crawl.** The map fills the screen above a scrolling crawl of the warnings you choose, for the area on screen, your alert zones or the states you pick. Add your own message after the alerts, up to 200 characters.
- **Breaking-alert takeover.** A new tornado warning (or any event you choose) flashes across the crawl bar the moment it is issued, with an optional chime. Emergencies always take over.
- **Warning counts** for tornado, severe thunderstorm and flash flood warnings, plus watches, in the corner of your choice.
- **Your own logo** next to the Spotter Tools Pro badge, in any corner and size.
- **Scenes.** Save views (camera, radar site and product, national radar or a model, and a Surface Obs readout) and let Broadcast Mode rotate through them on a timer, like a weather channel. Touch the map and the rotation waits, then picks back up.
- **Warning jump.** When a new warning comes in, the stream flies to it with the nearest radar up and its name as a title card, then goes back to your scenes when it is over. Choose the events, the product, how long to stay and a cooldown.
- **Radar control on stream.** Hotkeys for product, tilt, Prism and 3D, an on-map label with the product, site, tilt and scan time (or the model and valid time), and an optional product bar along the top edge.
- **Made for OBS.** A crawl-only output on a solid key colour, and an option to start in Broadcast Mode on launch so an unattended stream comes back after a restart. The screen stays awake while it is on.

> **All of it lives in Settings, Broadcast Mode,** on its own page with Output, Crawl & alerts, Look, Radar control, Scenes and Warning jump tabs.

## ProbSevere storm probabilities

NOAA's ProbSevere v3 outlines every storm and gives the chance it produces severe hail, severe wind or a tornado in the next hour.

- **Outlines coloured by the odds** of any severe weather, or hail, wind or tornado alone, with a percentage label on each storm.
- **ProbTor tags.** Any storm with a 10% or higher tornado chance gets a red **TOR** tag above its main number, so tornado odds show whatever the outlines are coloured by.
- **Tap a storm** for all four probabilities, the fields behind them, its motion and its downburst potential.
- **Options** for the hazard, a minimum probability (10% by default) and the tags. Your choices sync across devices.
- **Storms inside warnings** can be picked from the overlapping alerts list, alongside storm cell tracks.

> ProbSevere covers the lower 48 and updates about every 2 minutes. It is live only, so it steps aside in archive mode.

## Pop-out cameras

- **Float any live camera or chaser stream** over the map. Tap **Pop out** at the top of its sheet.
- **Drag it by the title bar, resize it from the corner** and tap it to bring it to the front.
- **Up to six at once on Windows and three on phones.** They stay put through dual view and tab switches and slide back on screen after you rotate or resize.

## Lightning coloured by strength

- **Every strike is coloured by its energy:** white for weak, yellow moderate, orange strong and red intense.
- **A new plus icon** if you prefer it to the bolt, in the lightning options with a colour key.
- **Tap a strike** for when it struck, its energy, the area it lit, how long it lasted, the nearby flash rate and trend, and how far it is from you.

## Alerts

- **A new Tornado Emergency alarm,** much louder than before and easy to tell apart from every other sound.
- **iPhone and iPad:** Tornado Warning and Severe Thunderstorm Warning sounds now play as intended instead of falling back to the default iOS sound.

## CarPlay

- **Radar loop.** Play the last 10 scans from the map buttons or the trip bar. It stops on its own after 5 minutes.
- **A cleaner route.** The route line no longer covers your car arrow, and the part you have already driven clears behind you.

## Also new

- **VIL in the Prism lens.** Add a VIL slice to judge hail potential, live, in loops and in archive mode (from March 2020).
- **Winds in mph** in the Inspector for surface obs, gusts, shear and model winds.
- **Settings is easier to get around.** Quick buttons scroll to their section instead of hiding the rest, Map and Radar are split into clearer sections, and search finds every setting again.
- **A faster map on slower Android devices.** Phones and car screens with a weak graphics chip now draw the map at a lighter resolution so panning stays smooth. Change it in Settings, Map rendering.
