# How to use what's new in 1.0.71

**Spotter Tools Pro  |  iPhone, iPad, Android and Windows**

This page walks through each new feature one step at a time. Every feature works the same way on a phone, a tablet and a Windows PC; where a step is different on Windows, it says so. On Windows, wherever this page says **tap**, just **click**.

> **Make sure you are on version 1.0.71 first.** On a phone, update from the App Store or Google Play. On Windows, open the Microsoft Store and choose Library, then Get updates. You can check your version in Settings, under About.

## Three places you will use a lot

- **The menu button.** The round button with three lines in the **bottom right corner** of the map. It opens a menu that includes **Map layers** and **Settings**.
- **Map layers.** Tap the menu button, then **Map layers**. Every layer has a switch that turns it on or off. On a phone, **tap the name of a layer** (not the switch) to open its options. On Windows, click the small **Options** button at the end of the row.
- **The radar chip.** When single site radar is on, a chip with your radar's name (for example **KTLX**) sits in the strip across the **top of the map**. Tap it to open the radar panel, where you pick the radar, the product and the new **Expert** button.

## Tornado ID (experimental)

Tornado ID puts a percentage on radar circulations: the estimated chance a tornado is on the ground there right now. It is off until you turn it on.

### Turn it on

1. Tap the **menu button** in the bottom right corner, then **Map layers**.
2. Make sure **Single Site Radar** is switched on.
3. Tap the words **Single Site Radar** to open its options. On Windows, click the **Options** button at the end of that row.
4. Switch on **Tornado ID (experimental)**. Underneath it says "Chance a tornado is on the ground, from radar".
5. Close the sheet and pick a radar with storms nearby.

### What you will see

- **A coloured circle with a percentage** on any circulation scoring 5% or more. **Amber** is 5 to 29%, **orange** is 30 to 59% and **red** is 60% and up.
- **No markers at all is normal** on a quiet day. It means nothing on that radar is scoring 5% or more.

### Read a marker

1. Tap a marker (click it on Windows). The **Tornado ID** card opens.
2. The big number is the score. The **Trend** line tells you whether it is rising, falling, steady or new this scan.
3. **Rotation** is how strong the spin is, **Debris signature** says whether the radar sees debris lofted into the air, and **Tracked** is how long the app has followed this circulation.
4. **Radar beam** says how high the radar is looking at that spot. If the beam is high, the card warns you, because a tornado near the ground can be missed or read low far from the radar.

A storm's cell card also shows a **Tornado ID** line while Tornado ID is on. Tap that line to open the full card.

### Use it on past storms

1. Tap the radar chip at the top of the map, then **Archive** (it reads **Archive…** on a phone).
2. Pick the date and time of a past event and tap **View archive**.
3. Your phone or computer now scores the scan itself. Give it a few seconds; markers appear when it finishes.
4. In an archived loop, press pause on the frame you want scored.

### Turn it off

Go back to **Map layers**, open the **Single Site Radar** options and switch **Tornado ID (experimental)** off.

> **Tornado ID is an estimate, not a warning.** It can miss a tornado and it can mark a storm that never produces one. Always follow official National Weather Service warnings.

## Expert Mode: up to three radars in one picture

When a storm sits between radars, the closest radar may only see its upper part. Expert mode lets the neighbouring radars fill in, so every spot on the map is drawn by the radar with the best view of it.

### Turn it on

1. Make sure single site radar is on and a radar is loaded.
2. Tap the **radar chip** at the top of the map to open the radar panel.
3. Tap **Expert**. It sits between the radar name and the product name, and turns blue when it is on.
4. A message confirms it: "Expert mode on. Tap another radar pill to add it to the image."

### Add and remove radars

1. Tap another **radar pill** on the map (the small labels like **KFWS** that mark each radar). It joins the picture.
2. Tap one more if you like. The limit is **three radars**, counting the one you started with.
3. Keep tapping along a line of storms if you want: past three, the radar you picked first rolls out to make room.
4. To take a radar out, **tap its pill again**, or tap the **x** on its number in the radar panel.

### What you will see

- **Radars in the picture glow bright** with a gold number badge. Your original radar is **1**.
- **Every other radar on the map dims**, so it is clear which ones are in play.
- **The radar panel shows numbered tokens** for each radar and a **+ Tap a radar** space for any open slot.
- The radar chip at the top shows how many radars are layered, for example **KMAF +2**.

### When the extra radars step aside

Expert mode uses Level III products at the lowest tilt. If you do any of the following, the extra radars pause and the panel tells you why, and they come back as soon as you return:

- Switch to a **Level II** product
- Pick a **higher tilt**
- **Play a loop**
- Turn on **3D**

### Turn it off

Tap **Expert** again. It always starts fresh, so the extra radars are cleared and it will not come back on by itself after you restart the app.

> **Also in Settings.** Settings, **Radar Overlay** has an **Expert mode** switch and a list of the radars in the picture, each with a button to remove it.

## Precip Type: rain, snow, sleet or ice

Precip Type is a radar product that colours each part of the radar by what is falling: **rain**, **snow**, **sleet** or **freezing rain**.

### Turn it on

1. Make sure single site radar is on.
2. Tap the **radar chip** at the top of the map to open the radar panel.
3. Tap the **product** (for example **Reflectivity**) to open the product list.
4. Under **Base**, pick **Precip Type**.

### What you will see

- **A legend with four colour scales:** Rain, Snow, Sleet and Frz Rain. Stronger colours mean heavier precipitation, just like normal reflectivity.
- **The model time** at the right of the legend, for example "type: HRRR 14:15Z". The type updates every 15 minutes.
- **The inspector** shows a **TYPE** line under the reflectivity at any spot you check.

> **How it works:** the radar measures how heavy the precipitation is, and the HRRR weather model supplies what type it is, because a radar cannot see the temperature between the cloud and the ground. **Precip Type is not offered for radars outside the HRRR model's coverage** (Alaska, Hawaii, Guam and Puerto Rico).

### Turn it off

Open the product list again and pick any other product, such as **Reflectivity**.

## The new outlooks

The Outlooks layer has many new products for winter storms, week two hazards and the months ahead.

### Open the outlooks

1. Tap the **menu button**, then **Map layers**, and switch on **Outlooks**.
2. An **Outlooks** chip appears in the strip at the top of the map. Tap it to open the outlook chips.
3. Tap the **first chip** to choose a product. The chips after it change to match the product you picked.
4. Tap the **i** chip at any time for the legend and when that product is issued.

### What is new, and where to find it

- **Winter Weather** (shown as **Winter Wx**): pick a day (Day 1 to 3), then a threshold: **Snow > 4"**, **Snow > 8"**, **Snow > 12"** or **Ice > 0.25"**. The map shows the chance of reaching it. It is empty when no winter storm is expected.
- **WPC Hazards (Days 3-7)**: pick **Combined** or one hazard: Precipitation, Temperature, Wildfires, Soils or Flooding.
- **CPC Hazards (Days 8-14)**: the week two outlook. Pick **Combined** or one hazard, including **Prob. Extreme Heat** and **Rapid Onset Drought**.
- **Fire Weather**: the day chip now goes out to **Day 8**. Days 3 to 8 show the chance of critical fire weather.
- **Precip Forecast**: a new **Type** chip picks **Daily**, **Multi-Day** or **6-Hour** rainfall, then choose the period.
- **Winter Storm Severity**: a new chip shows the **Overall Impact** or one part of it: **Snow Amount**, **Snow Load**, **Ice Accumulation** or **Blowing Snow**.
- **CPC Climate**: besides 6-10 and 8-14 day, the period chip now has **Monthly** (next month) and **Seasonal** (next three months), for temperature or precipitation.
- **Drought**: besides the current **Drought Monitor**, pick the **Monthly Outlook** or **Seasonal Outlook** to see where drought is expected to improve, develop or hang on.

### Turn it off

Tap the **x** on the outlook panel, or switch **Outlooks** off in Map layers.

## Live Cameras: show only live video or only stills

1. Tap the **menu button**, then **Map layers**.
2. Tap the words **Live Cams** (on Windows, click its **Options** button). The **Live Cameras** sheet opens.
3. At the top, the **Filter** card shows how many cameras are on the map right now.
4. Choose **All**, **Live** or **Stills**. Each choice shows how many cameras it has.

The app remembers your choice. Pick **All** to go back to showing everything.

## Hurricane Hunters: export a flight

1. Tap the **menu button**, then **Map layers**, and tap the words **Aviation** (on Windows, click its **Options** button).
2. In the list of flights, press the **chart** button next to a flight ("Graph this flight"). Or tap a flight's track on the map and press **FLIGHT GRAPHS** on its card.
3. On the graphs screen, press the **share** button (named **Export**).
4. Choose **Share image** for a two page picture brief, or **Share data** for spreadsheet files.
5. Your phone's or PC's share menu opens. Send it anywhere you like: a message, email, social media or your files.

> The image uses your own display units. The data files keep the aircraft's own units (knots, millibars and Celsius) so they match the official bulletins.

## Archive loops wait for you

1. Open an archive time as usual (radar chip, then **Archive**).
2. If a loop is up, it now **stops on the oldest frame** and waits. Press **Play** on the animation bar when you are ready.
3. Tap **Return to live** on the **ARCHIVE** banner to go back. The loop closes and the live radar and sweep come back.

## Clean up the national radar mosaic

The Reflectivity filter now also works on the **National Radar** layer, not only single site radar.

1. Tap the **menu button**, then **Settings**.
2. Open **Radar Overlay**, then **Gate filters**.
3. Drag the left end of the **Reflectivity** slider up to hide weaker returns. Tap the reset button to go back to the full range.

## Windows only: smoother on slower PCs

If the map stutters on a high resolution screen, especially a laptop without a separate graphics card:

1. Click the **menu button**, then **Settings**.
2. Find the **Labels & Roads** card and open **Map rendering**.
3. Leave it on **Auto** first. If panning still stutters, choose **Performance**.
4. Still not smooth? Turn on **Scale the whole app**, then **close and reopen** Spotter Tools Pro. Text and menus look a little softer, but everything moves more smoothly. It has no effect if your Windows display scaling is set to 100%.

The line under these options shows the resolution the map is drawn at and which graphics chip your PC is using.
