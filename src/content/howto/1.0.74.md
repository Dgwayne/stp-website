# How to use what's new in 1.0.74

**Spotter Tools Pro  |  iPhone, iPad, Android and Windows**

This page walks through each new feature one step at a time. On Windows, wherever this page says **tap**, just **click**.

> **Make sure you are on version 1.0.74 first.** On a phone, update from the App Store or Google Play. On Windows, open the Microsoft Store and choose Library, then Get updates. You can check your version in Settings, under About.

> **Coming from 1.0.71 on Android or Windows?** This update also brings Settings Sync, the cleaner national radar and national Precip Type. Their step by step guide is at [spottertools.pro/1.0.72/howto](/1.0.72/howto)

## CarPlay (iPhone)

### Open it

1. Connect your iPhone to a car or head unit with CarPlay.
2. Tap the **Spotter Tools Pro** icon on the CarPlay screen. It works even if the app is closed on your phone.
3. The map opens on live radar from the nearest radar site, following your car.

### Choose what you see

- **Layers** (top left): pick **Reflectivity**, **Velocity**, **Storm-relative velocity**, **Correlation coefficient** or **National composite**; turn **Warnings** on or off; set **Map style** to Automatic, Dark or Light; and turn **Spoken directions** on or off.
- **Sites** (top left): **Automatic** follows the nearest radar as you drive, or pick a radar from the list.
- The buttons on the side of the map zoom in and out, pan, re-center on your car, and step back through earlier scans.

### Get directions in the car

1. Tap the **search** button (the magnifying glass) and type a town, ZIP code or address. Or tap **Go** for your **Recent** places and **Nearby storms**.
2. Pick a result to see the route, then tap **Go**.
3. Follow the turn cards and spoken prompts. Tap the speaker button to mute the voice.
4. Tap **End** to finish. When you arrive, an **Arrived** alert shows on the screen.

A trip you start on your phone appears in the car too, and **End** in the car ends it on the phone.

> **Searches find towns, ZIP codes and street addresses,** not business names. Keep your eyes on the road.

## Android Auto

### Open it

1. Connect your phone to Android Auto in your car (or start Android Auto on a compatible head unit).
2. Open the app launcher on the car screen and tap **Spotter Tools Pro**. It is listed with the weather apps.
3. The map opens on live radar from the nearest radar site, following your car.

### Read the screen

- **The Live radar card** shows the radar, product and how old the scan is (for example **KMAF · REF · 2 min ago**), and under it the nearest warning in its own colour, or how soon an approaching storm reaches you.
- **Warning polygons** cover the whole country, and **storm cells** show their forecast track. **Storm reports** appear as lettered markers (T for tornado, H for hail and so on).

### The buttons along the top

- **History** (the clock): steps back one scan per tap, up to five. After 30 seconds it goes back to live on its own.
- **Warning** icon: shows or hides the warning polygons.
- **Layers**: pick **Reflectivity**, **Velocity**, **Storm-relative velocity**, **Correlation coefficient** or **National composite**, and set **Map style** (Auto follows the car, Always Midnight, or Always light).
- **Sites** (the tower): **Nearest radar (auto)** switches as you drive, or pick a radar from the list.

### Tap something on the map

1. Tap a storm cell, a storm report or a warning.
2. A detail card shows how far it is from you and, for a storm, where it is heading and when it reaches you.
3. Tap **Navigate** to send that spot to Google Maps, Waze or your car's navigation.

> **Android Auto shows the latest scan, not a loop,** because Google does not allow animation in Weather apps while driving. Warnings the phone app alerts you to also pop up on the car screen while connected, as long as alerts are on in the phone app.

## Turn-by-turn directions on your phone

Phones and tablets only; not on Windows.

### Start a trip

Any of these works:

- **Long-press** the map where you want to go, then tap **Directions here**.
- Tap the **menu button** (bottom right), then **Search location**, type a place, and tap the **car** button next to it.
- With the search box empty, tap a place under **Recent destinations**.

### Before you go

1. The preview shows the drive time, the distance and the main roads.
2. Tap **Start**. Or **Cancel** to back out.

### On the road

- **The banner across the top** shows the next turn, how far away it is, the road, and your arrival time.
- **Spoken prompts** lower your music instead of stopping it. Tap **Voice** on the banner to mute them (it reads **Muted**); the app remembers your choice.
- **Missed a turn?** The app reroutes on its own.
- The map follows you at street level and the screen stays on. On Android, a **Directions on** notification shows while a trip runs.

### End the trip

Tap **End** on the banner. When you arrive, the banner says **Arrived** and the trip ends by itself shortly after.

## Live Chaser Positions

See which featured storm chasers are streaming live right now, right where they are.

### Turn it on

1. Tap the **menu button**, then **Map layers**.
2. Switch on **Live Chaser Positions**.

Or open **Live Chasers** from the menu and switch on **Show on map**.

### What you will see

- **A marker for each featured chaser** who is live on YouTube and sharing their position on Spotter Network in the last 20 minutes. If nobody is streaming, the map shows none.
- **Partner chasers** have a larger gold-ringed marker with their photo and a gold name, and a gold **PARTNER** tag at the top of the Live Chasers list.
- **Tap a marker** to watch that chaser's live stream.

The layer is hidden in archive mode, because positions are only live.

## Share button

1. Get the screen how you want it: the map, a storm, a forecast, anything.
2. Tap **Share** in the bottom bar (between Dual and Hide).
3. The app captures the screen and adds a footer below it with the Spotter Tools Pro logo, spottertools.pro and the date and time.
4. Your phone's or PC's share menu opens. Send it to a message, social media, email or your photos.

If the map is still loading, give it a second and try again.

## Low and mid level rotation together

1. Turn on the national radar: tap the **quick layers button** (just above the menu button), then **National**.
2. Tap the **National radar** chip at the top of the map, then the **product** chip to open the product list.
3. Under **Rotation**, tap **Low + Mid** in the **Height** row.
4. Tap a **Time window** (Now, 30m, 1 hr and so on). The list closes and the map shows both levels.

Low level rotation (0 to 2 km) is drawn in warm colours and mid level rotation (3 to 6 km) in violet. **Where both levels are spinning, the map draws diagonal stripes of the two.** The legend shows both scales and a striped **Both levels** swatch.
