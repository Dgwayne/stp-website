# How to use what's new in 1.0.75

**Spotter Tools Pro  |  iPhone, iPad, Android and Windows**

This page walks through each new feature one step at a time. On Windows, wherever this page says **tap**, just **click**.

> **Make sure you are on version 1.0.75 first.** On a phone, update from the App Store or Google Play. On Windows, open the Microsoft Store and choose Library, then Get updates. You can check your version in Settings, under About.

## The new command bar

Everything starts from the bar floating at the top of the map.

1. Tap a tab to switch what the map shows: **Single Site**, **National**, **Models**, **Satellite** or **Outlooks**.
2. The controls for that product (product picker, time, opacity and so on) hang right under the bar.
3. Tap the **red x** in a product's controls to turn that product off.

Search is in the bar too, and so is your beacon status. On a phone the bar is one compact row with a small icon over each tab name.

## Prism lens

### Open it

1. Turn on single site radar (the **Single Site** tab) and pick a radar.
2. In the radar controls under the bar, tap **Prism**.
3. A circle opens in the middle of the map. Over reflectivity it shows velocity; over any other product it shows reflectivity.

### Use it

- **Move it:** drag the circle over the storm you are watching.
- **Resize it:** drag the small knob on its edge. On a phone you can also pinch inside the circle; on Windows, hold **Ctrl** and use the scroll wheel.
- **Change what it shows:** tap the label on a slice (for example **VEL 2m**). Pick a product, choose **Split** 1, 2, 3 or 4 to show several products side by side, and turn **Dark backdrop** on or off.
- **Close it:** tap the **x** on the circle, or tap **Prism** again.

The label on each slice shows how old its scan is, and turns amber if it is more than 2 minutes behind the map. The lens keeps working while a loop plays and in archive mode.

> **The lens hides itself in 3D, in dual view and while the map is tilted,** and comes back when you leave them.

## 3D radar: height lines, sunlight and hail cores

1. On single site radar, tap the **3D** chip.
2. A row of 3D options appears under the radar controls. Under **Show**, tap a chip to turn it on or off: **Height grid** (height lines on the storms with numbers and echo-top tags), **Sunlight** (light and shadows), **Cores** (hail cores showing through the storm), and **Debris** and **Rotation** (the tornado highlights).
3. Play the loop to see up to 6 hours of 3D. The small 3D time under the scan time shows how old the volume is.

## Warnings in the loop

Nothing to turn on. With warnings showing, play any single site or national loop: each frame shows the warnings that were in effect at that moment. Watch a warning appear when it was issued, change shape with updates, and go away when it ended. The newest frame is always live.

## Inspector on more products

1. Open **National**, **Models**, or the mesoanalysis or observations panel.
2. Tap **Inspector** in its controls. A crosshair appears in the middle of the map.
3. Move the map: the readout shows the value under the crosshair. Tap **Inspector** again to hide it.

## Models: follow the latest run

1. Open **Models** and tap the run chip (for example **18Z Fri · Latest**).
2. Pick **Latest** to always follow new runs, or pick an older run to pin it. A pinned run shows a pin icon, and a **New run** badge tells you when a newer run is out.

## Surface Obs stations

1. Tap the **menu button**, then **Map layers**, and switch on **Surface Obs**.
2. On the map, tap the **Stations** chip (next to **Readings**) and choose which stations to show: **Airports**, **Fire weather (RAWS)**, **Gov & mesonet** and **Citizen (CWOP)**. Each type has its own dot colour.
3. In **Readings**, try the new **Wind Gust** and **Relative Humidity**.
4. Tap any station for its details.

More stations appear as you zoom in.

## City density

1. Open **Settings** and find **City labels**.
2. Under **City density**, pick **Sparse**, **Normal**, **Dense** or **Maximum**.

State names and major cities always show, whatever you pick.

## Loop export on Windows

1. Open the loop export (the export button next to the loop controls) and choose the **Full** size to export at your screen's full resolution.
2. When it finishes, the file is saved to your Videos or Pictures folder in **Spotter Tools Pro**. Click **Show in folder** to open it.
