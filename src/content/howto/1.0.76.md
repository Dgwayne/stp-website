# How to use what's new in 1.0.76

**Spotter Tools Pro  |  iPhone, iPad, Android and Windows**

This page walks through each new feature one step at a time. On Windows, wherever this page says **tap**, just **click**.

> **Make sure you are on version 1.0.76 first.** On a phone, update from the App Store or Google Play. On Windows, open the Microsoft Store and choose Library, then Get updates. You can check your version in Settings, under About.

## Broadcast Mode (Windows)

A full-screen view made for streaming: the live map, a scrolling alert crawl, warning counts, a clock and your own logo.

![Broadcast Mode on stream: the alert crawl along the bottom, warning counts top right and the radar label bottom left](/images/howto-1.0.76/broadcast-stream.jpg)

### Start and stop it

1. Open the map menu and choose **Broadcast Mode (F11)**, or just press **F11**.
2. The map goes full screen with the crawl along the bottom.
3. Press **Esc** or **F11** to leave. Your window comes back the way it was.

You can also start it from **Settings**, **Broadcast**, then **Start Broadcast Mode**.

### Set it up

Go to **Settings**, tap the **Broadcast** button at the top, then **Broadcast settings**. Each tab has its own **Reset this tab** button.

- **Output:** full screen, or crawl only on a solid color for OBS (add a Chroma Key filter on that color in OBS). Turn on **Start in Broadcast Mode** if you leave a stream running on its own, so it comes back by itself after a restart.
- **Crawl & alerts:** which area the crawl covers (the map on screen, your alert zones or states you pick), which alerts it carries, your own message after the alerts, breaking-alert takeovers with an optional chime, and the warning counts box.
- **Look:** colors, size, the label on the crawl, your own logo, the clock and the legend.
- **Radar control:** the on-map radar label, hotkeys and the product bar.
- **Scenes** and **Warning jump:** see below.

![The Crawl & alerts tab, with a live preview of the crawl at the top](/images/howto-1.0.76/broadcast-crawl-tab.jpg)

### Change the radar on stream

With hotkeys on, these keys work while Broadcast Mode is up. Nothing shows on screen when you press them, and a Stream Deck can send them too. You can change any key in **Radar control**.

- **R** reflectivity, **V** velocity, **S** storm relative velocity, **C** correlation coefficient
- **Z** differential reflectivity, **K** specific differential phase, **H** hydrometeor classification
- **[** and **]** tilt down and up
- **P** Prism lens on or off, **3** 3D on or off

If no radar site is up, a product key brings up the radar nearest the middle of the map.

![The Radar control tab, where every hotkey can be changed](/images/howto-1.0.76/broadcast-radar-tab.jpg)

### Scenes: let the stream run itself

A scene is a saved view: where the map is, the radar or model on it, and an optional Surface Obs readout. Broadcast Mode can rotate through your scenes on a timer.

1. Move the map to a view you want and set up the radar the way you like it.
2. In the **Scenes** tab tap **Save current map view as a scene**, or press **A** while Broadcast Mode is on.
3. Repeat for each view. Drag scenes to reorder them, and set how long each one stays up.
4. Press **G** to start or stop the rotation. **N** goes to the next scene, **B** back, and **M** pauses.

Touching the map or pressing a radar key holds the current scene. The rotation picks up again after the idle time you chose. When you stop, the map goes back to how it was before.

### Warning jump

When a new warning comes in, the stream flies to it with the nearest radar up and the warning's name as a title card, then goes back to your scenes when it is over.

1. Open the **Warning jump** tab and turn it on.
2. Pick which warnings jump, whether it covers the crawl's area or anywhere, and which radar product to show.
3. Under **Stay on warnings**, pick how long it stays: until the warnings expire, a set time, or until you press **X**.

Only warnings issued in the last 10 minutes count as new, so starting in the middle of an outbreak never replays old ones.

## ProbSevere

NOAA's ProbSevere outlines each storm it is tracking and gives the chance it produces severe hail, severe wind or a tornado in the next hour.

### Turn it on

1. Tap the menu button, then **Map layers**.
2. Turn on **ProbSevere** (in the list just below Outlooks).
3. Tap the row itself, not the switch, to open its options.

![ProbSevere outlines over national radar, each with its percentage](/images/howto-1.0.76/probsevere-map.jpg)

### Read it

- **The outline color and the number** show the chance of any severe weather, or of just hail, wind or tornado if you pick one under **Colour storms by**.
- **A red TOR tag** above the number means the storm has a 10% or higher chance of a tornado, whatever the outlines are colored by. Turn the tags off under **Tornado tags**.
- **Tap a storm or its number** for the full breakdown: all four chances, how fast it is moving and how far it is from you, hail size, downburst potential, rotation and lightning.
- **Hide storms below** a percentage to keep the map clean. Most tracked storms sit near 0%, so the default is 10%.

![The storm card: all four chances and the fields behind them](/images/howto-1.0.76/probsevere-card.jpg#w=450)

> **Storm inside a warning?** Tap the spot and choose the storm from the list of everything under your finger.

## Pop-out cameras

1. Open any live camera or a chaser's stream as usual.
2. Tap **Pop out** at the top of its sheet. The video moves into a small window over the map.
3. **Drag** the window by its title bar, **resize** it from the corner, and tap it to bring it to the front.
4. Tap the **X** to close it.

You can have up to six windows on Windows and three on a phone, and they stay put through dual view and tab switches.

## Lightning by strength

1. Turn on **Lightning** in **Map layers**, then tap the row to open its options.
2. Under **Icon**, pick **Bolt** or **Plus**. The key below shows the colors: white weak, yellow moderate, orange strong and red intense.
3. Tap any strike on the map for its card: when it struck, its energy, the area it lit, how long it lasted, the flash rate nearby and how far it is from you.

![The lightning flash card and the new icon options](/images/howto-1.0.76/lightning-card-options.jpg#w=360)

> The color is how bright the flash looked to the GOES satellite, not its current. The satellite sees in-cloud and cloud-to-ground lightning alike.

## Also new

- **VIL in the Prism lens.** Open the Prism lens, tap a slice's label and choose **VIL** to judge hail potential. It works live, in loops and in archive mode back to March 2020.
- **Tornado Emergency alarm.** There is nothing to set up. If you already get Tornado Emergency alerts, you will hear the new, louder alarm.
- **CarPlay radar loop.** On the CarPlay map tap **Play** to loop the last 10 scans, and **Stop** to go back to live. On a trip the button sits in the bar next to Layers.
- **Settings.** The buttons at the top now scroll to their section. Use the search box to find any setting by name.
- **Slower Android devices.** If panning the map feels slow, go to **Settings**, **Map rendering** and choose **Performance**. It takes effect the next time you open the app.
