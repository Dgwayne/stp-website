# How to use what's new in 1.0.72

**Spotter Tools Pro  |  iPhone, iPad, Android and Windows**

This page walks through each new feature one step at a time. Everything works the same way on a phone, a tablet and a Windows PC; where a step is different on Windows, it says so. On Windows, wherever this page says **tap**, just **click**.

> **Make sure you are on version 1.0.72 first.** On a phone, update from the App Store or Google Play. On Windows, open the Microsoft Store and choose Library, then Get updates. You can check your version in Settings, under About.

## Settings Sync: the same setup on every device

Sign in to a free Spotter Tools Pro account and your settings follow you to every phone, tablet and PC you use.

### Sign in and turn it on

1. Open **Settings** (the Settings tab at the bottom of the screen).
2. Find **Spotter Tools Pro Account**. You can also type **sync** in the Settings search box to jump there.
3. Tap **Sign In or Create Account**.
4. Choose **Continue with Google**, **Continue with Apple**, or type an email address and password. New here? Tap **No account yet? Create one** first.
5. Back in Settings, **Sync settings across devices** is already switched on. The line under it says when your settings last synced.
6. Do the same on your other devices, signing in to the same account.

### The first time on a device that already has settings

If your account already has settings saved from another device, the app asks **Use your saved settings?**

- Tap **Use saved settings** to make this device match your others.
- Tap **Keep this device's** to keep what this device has and send it to your account instead.

Settings that only exist on one side are kept either way.

![The prompt you see the first time on a device that already has its own settings](/images/howto-1.0.72/sync-prompt.png#w=412)

### Choose what syncs

1. In the account card, tap **Choose what syncs**.
2. Untick any group you want this device to keep to itself:
   - **Map style & theme**
   - **Layers & layer options**
   - **Radar** (gate filters, palettes, favorite radars and more)
   - **Alerts & zones**
   - **Marker & icon sizes** (your marker goes everywhere; sizes are only shared between phones and tablets, or between computers)
   - **Home location & other**

A handy setup: untick **Layers & layer options** on your PC so it can show a different set of layers than your phone.

![Choose what syncs, with Layers unticked so this device keeps its own layers](/images/howto-1.0.72/sync-categories.png#w=412)

### When changes show up

- **Your changes are sent within seconds** of making them.
- **Your other devices pick them up** the next time you open the app or come back to it. On Windows, switching back to the window after a minute or more is enough.
- **Want it now?** Tap **Sync now** in the account card.
- Changes never land while you are in the middle of using the app.

### What never syncs

Your passwords and Spotter Network sign-in, your beacon, **Show me on the map**, the alerts on and off switch, Chaser Mode, and screen and window settings always stay on the device they were set on.

### Turn it off

- **On one device:** switch off **Sync settings across devices**. That device keeps its own settings from then on.
- **Everywhere:** tap **Choose what syncs**, then **Delete saved settings from my account**. This removes the copy stored in your account and turns sync off on that device. Your devices keep their current settings. Turn sync off on your other devices first, or they will save their settings to your account again.

## The cleaner national radar

The national radar now opens on **Base Reflectivity (QC)**, which removes birds, insects and ground clutter. You don't need to do anything; every device switches once, automatically.

### Switch between the clean and original versions

1. Turn on the national radar: tap the **quick layers button** (the round button just above the menu button in the bottom right corner), then **National**. Or use the menu button, **Map layers**, then switch on **National Radar**.
2. Tap the **National radar** chip in the strip at the top of the map.
3. Tap the **product** chip (it shows the product name and a small arrow). The **Composite Products** list opens.
4. Under **Reflectivity**, pick:
   - **Base Reflectivity (QC)** for the clean version
   - **Base Reflectivity** for the original, unfiltered version

The app remembers your choice.

![Where to switch the national radar product, shown on Windows. On a phone the National radar chip sits in the same strip at the top of the map.](/images/howto-1.0.72/national-picker.jpg)

> **Want a 3, 6 or 12 hour national loop?** Those lengths are only offered on **Base Reflectivity**. The QC version and Precip Type loop up to 2 hours.

## Precip Type on the national radar

Colours the whole national radar by what is falling: **rain**, **snow**, **sleet** or **freezing rain**.

### Turn it on

1. Turn on the national radar and open its **product** list, as in steps 1 to 3 above.
2. Under **Reflectivity**, pick **Precip Type**.

### What you will see

- **A legend titled PRECIP TYPE (dBZ)** with four colour scales: Rain, Snow, Sleet and Frz Rain. Stronger colours mean heavier precipitation.
- **The model time** at the right of the legend, for example "type: HRRR 14:15Z". The type updates every 15 minutes.
- **The crosshair inspector** shows the type at any spot, for example **Precip Type · Snow**, with the reflectivity.

![The national radar on Precip Type during real rain across Kansas on October 1, with the four-scale legend at the bottom](/images/howto-1.0.72/national-precip.jpg)

> **Precip Type covers the lower 48 states,** where the HRRR model runs. In Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico you see regular reflectivity instead.

### Turn it off

Open the product list again and pick **Base Reflectivity (QC)** or any other product.

## Tropical model tracks

Nothing new to switch on: the fixes apply automatically. If you have never turned the model tracks on, here is how.

1. Tap the **menu button**, then **Map layers**, and switch on **Tropical**.
2. Tap the word **Tropical** to open its options (on Windows, click the **Options** button at the end of the row).
3. Switch on **Model tracks**.

You now see each model's newest track from the last 12 hours, coloured by model, with the legend in the same sheet. Storms near the 180 degree line in the Pacific no longer draw lines around the globe.

## Single site Precip Type and archive mode

Single site **Precip Type** now works on live radar only. When you open an archive time, the radar switches to **Reflectivity** and Precip Type leaves the product list until you return to live. To use it again after returning to live, pick **Precip Type** from the product list.
